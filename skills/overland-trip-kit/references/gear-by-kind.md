# Gear by kind

Each kind of gear goes through three steps: set up, use, pack up. A careless trip skips one. When you hand a person a checklist, walk the kinds that fit their rig through these three steps.

Kinds only, never brands. If the person asks what to buy: name the kind and what it does for the trip, ask what they already carry, and say it is their call. Never name a brand or a product, never say a thing is needed to be safe, and never say a piece of gear "works" until they have used it.

"Rig" says where it fits: truck (living outside), van (an inside, with tanks), or both.

## Water

| Kind | Rig | Set up | Use | Pack up |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Drinking and cooking jug | truck | Fill in town. | Drinking and cooking; it sets the nights before the town run. | Keep it capped. |
| Fresh water tank | van | Fill in town. | Sink, shower and drinking water. | Built in. |
| Extra water containers | both | Fill in town. | Carry, then pour into the jug or tank. | Empty and stow. More water means more nights before a town run, and more weight. |
| Hand-wash jug and soap | both | Set on the table before cooking. | Wash hands before cooking and after the toilet. | Empty, dry, stow with the soap. |
| Water filter | both | Attach to the jug or the creek line. | Treat natural water. Natural water is untested: filter or boil. | Drain, dry, stow. |
| Roof shower tank | truck | Fill in town; it warms on the roof. | A shower: one rig we know uses about 2 gallons, so measure yours. | Nothing to pack; it rides on the roof. |
| Gray water tank | van | Built in. | Collects sink and shower water. | Empty at a dump station only. A full tank means a dump stop on the town run. |
| Wet bath and cassette toilet | van | Built in. | Shower and toilet inside; they use fresh water and fill the tanks. | Empty the cassette at a dump station only. |

## Power

| Kind | Rig | Set up | Use | Pack up |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| House batteries | both | Built in. | Run the fridge, lights and Starlink dish. Check the level at sunset and at sunrise. | Built in. |
| Fixed solar panels | both | Park in the sun; shade means less charge. | Charge the batteries by day. | Nothing to pack; check they are clear before driving. |
| Portable battery and portable panels | truck | Set the panels out in the sun and connect. | Carry the Starlink dish and spare the fridge. | Fold the panels and stow before driving or when the wind rises. |
| DC-to-DC charger | van | Built in. | Charges the bank from the alternator while driving. | Built in. |
| Inverter | both | Built in. | Makes household power from the batteries; it is a load too. | Built in. |
| Battery monitor | both | Built in. | Shows the charge level for the sunset and sunrise check. | Built in. |
| 12-volt fridge | truck | Plug in; it runs off the batteries. | Keeps food cold; a dead battery spoils food. | Latch the lid before driving. |
| Compressor fridge | van | Built in. | Keeps food cold; it draws the bank. | Latch the door. |
| Starlink dish | both | Needs open sky and power; set it up away from tall trees. | Connection at camp; a steady power draw. Trees and canyon walls block it. | Pack the dish and its cable. |

## Camp kitchen and living

| Kind | Rig | Set up | Use | Pack up |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Camp stove and a full fuel canister | truck | Set it on the tailgate or table, out of the wind; attach the canister. | Breakfast and dinner. | Let it cool, remove the canister, stow. |
| Galley (sink, counter, cooktop) | van | Built in. | Cook inside; sink water goes to the gray tank; the cooktop draws the battery if it is electric. | Wipe down; latch every cabinet. |
| Camp table and chairs | both | Set up the kitchen beside the rig. | Cook and sit. | Fold and pack. |
| Cooler | both | Set it beside the table. | Holds cold food. | Empty, drain, stow. |
| Lantern | both | Hang at dusk. | Evening chores without draining the headlamp. A lantern left on drains its battery. | Pack. |
| Headlamp | both | Charge at home; wear at dusk. | Camp chores after dark. | Switch off, stow, recharge. |
| Awning | both | Roll out and stake. | Shade or rain cover. | Stow before driving or when the wind rises; wind can damage one left out. |
| Fire ring, shovel and water | both | Use an existing ring only; keep any fire small; keep a shovel and water beside it. | A fire only where the forest's posted orders and the camp's rules allow one. | Drown it, stir it, feel it cold. Pack out any trash from the ring. |

## Sleep

| Kind | Rig | Set up | Use | Pack up |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Truck-bed shell with roof tent | truck | Stake out, pop up the shell, open the roof tent. | The bed: level and wind matter. | Pop down, latch, check the shell before driving. A tent left up when driving is damage. |
| Van bed | van | Made up inside. | Sleep inside; level still matters. | Latch the cabinets. |
| Sleeping bag and pad, by temperature rating | both | Choose at home against the real low for the dates. | Sleep. | Air and stow. Spring, summer and fall only. |
| Air heater | van | Built in. | Takes the edge off a cool spring or fall night. | Built in. |

## Recovery and the rig

| Kind | Rig | Set up | Use | Pack up |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Traction boards | both | Pull from the rack; dig out, set under the wheel. | The way out of a soft spot. | Clean off the mud, strap back on the rack. |
| Shovel | both | Keep it within reach. | Dig out a stuck wheel; keep it beside any fire. | Stow. |
| Tow strap and rated recovery points | both | Check at home that you know where the points are. | Recovery. | Stow. |
| Winch | both | Built into the bumper. | Self-recovery when stuck. | Built on the rig. |
| Air compressor and tire repair kit | both | Air down at the dirt. | Drive; air up at the pavement. | Stow the kit. A flat without one ends the day. |
| Spare tire, jack and lug wrench | both | Check at home that you can get the spare off the rig. | Use on a flat. | Back on the rig. |
| Jerry can | both | Check it seals. | Extra fuel only if the rig is built for it; count it in the gas math. | Stow upright and sealed. |

## Safety and hygiene

| Kind | Rig | Set up | Use | Pack up |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First-aid kit | both | Check and restock before the trip. | There when needed. Any medicine the person takes, plus spare days. | Check and stow. |
| Fire extinguisher | both | Mounted where you can reach it. | There when needed. | Check the gauge. |
| Bear-resistant food storage | both | Stow food at dusk. | Where wildlife is around, food, trash and anything with a smell go in a hard-sided vehicle or a wildlife-resistant container, day and night. | Open in the morning; stow. |
| Pack-out toilet system | both | Set up away from camp, water and trails. | Pack out the waste. | Seal and carry. |
| Trash bags | both | Aboard. | Carry all trash out. | Never leave it at camp or at a trailhead. |
